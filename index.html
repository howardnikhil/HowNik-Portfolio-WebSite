Engineering Approach

I Build Across the Stack — Literally.

Most projects stall at handoffs between software, circuit design, and mechanical packaging. I eliminate those boundaries.

I work at the active intersection of electronics, robotics, artificial intelligence, and physical product engineering. Rather than remaining isolated within a single domain, my work moves fluidly across every physical and digital layer — from early mathematical architecture and schematic capture to embedded firmware, distributed middleware, AI inference, and CNC/additive fabrication.

I am drawn to systems where hardware and software cannot exist independently. Whether that means engineering an autonomous 4-legged robot that balances against perturbation, developing sub-decimeter indoor localization anchors, creating an open-source robotics runtime, or packaging CubeSat avionics inside a strict 100mm form factor — I build working systems.

Through founding HowNik Technologies and steering technical vision at FirstDay AI, I have learned that true engineering credibility comes from one thing: the discipline to break down complex, multi-disciplinary problems and build working machines.
